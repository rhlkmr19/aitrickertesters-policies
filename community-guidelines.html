Community Guidelines

Rules for Group Chat in AiTricker Testers

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

💬 Text only 🚫 No links or promotion 🚩 Report & Block 🛡️ Moderated

Group Chat exists for one reason: to help the members of your own testing group finish their 14 days successfully. Ask a question, report a problem you found in an app, tell the group you have checked in. That is it.

⚠️ Accepting these Guidelines

You must accept these Guidelines inside the app before you can send your first message. By sending a message you confirm that you have read and agree to the rules below, and to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

1

What Group Chat is

💬 How it works

  • Group members only. Only the testers of your own active group can read or send messages. There is no public chat, no private one-to-one messaging, and no way to be contacted by a stranger.
  • Text only. No photos, videos, files, voice notes or attachments can be sent — the feature does not support them at all.
  • Short messages. Each message is limited to 100 characters.
  • A waiting time between messages. After you send a message you must wait a short cooldown before sending the next one. This keeps the chat readable and makes flooding impossible.
  • Only while the group runs. Chat is available while your group is active. When the group finishes, the chat closes and all of its messages are permanently deleted.
2

The rules

  1. Keep it about testing. Questions, help, feedback and check-in updates for the apps in your group.
  2. Be respectful. No abuse, insults, swearing, threats, harassment, sexual content, hate speech, or attacks on anyone's religion, caste, gender, nationality or identity.
  3. No links or contact details. No URLs, no invite links, no phone numbers, no email addresses, no social media handles or usernames.
  4. No promotion or advertising. Do not advertise channels, groups, services, websites or other apps. Do not ask people to subscribe, follow or "sub for sub".
  5. No money, no selling, no "DM me". Do not buy, sell, or offer paid services. Do not ask people to message you privately or on another platform.
  6. No personal information. Do not post your own or anyone else's private details, and never share passwords, OTPs or payment information.
  7. No scams or illegal activity. No fake offers, no earning schemes, no remote-access tools, no anything that breaks the law.
  8. No impersonation. Do not pretend to be the admin, a Google employee, or another user.
  9. One account, one person. Do not use the chat to organise cheating of the testing rules.
3

How we enforce this

Three layers, in this order:

1️⃣ Automatic check before sending

Every message is checked on our server before anyone can see it. Abuse, links, contact details, prices and known promotional phrases are refused. A refused message is simply not delivered — you are told about it and you can rewrite it. A refused message is not a punishment: there is no warning, no Trust Score change and no effect on your testing.

2️⃣ Reports from members

Any member can report a message. When enough different members report the same message, it is hidden from the group immediately and sent to the admin for review. You can also block an individual member so that you never see their messages again — that choice is yours alone, and the other person is not told.

3️⃣ Admin action

The admin reviews reported messages and can:

  • delete the message,
  • suspend your access to chat for 1 to 14 days, or for the remaining life of the group,
  • dismiss the report and restore the message if it was reported unfairly.

Serious or repeated violations can also lead to action on your account under our Terms & Conditions. A chat suspension does not stop you from testing, checking in, or earning — you keep taking part in your group normally.

🔎 What the admin can and cannot see

The admin does not read group chats. Messages reach the admin only when members report them. This is deliberate — moderation should follow a complaint, not surveillance.

4

How to report or block someone

📱 Inside the app

  • Report a message: press and hold the message → Report.
  • Block a member: press and hold any of their messages → Block. Their messages disappear from your chat.
  • Unblock someone: chat screen → menu (⋮) → Blocked members.

✉️ Serious matters

For anything urgent or serious — threats, harassment that continues, child safety concerns, or illegal content — email us directly. Please include the group name and roughly when it happened.

Email: aitrickertesters@gmail.com

Response time: within 24 hours

5

Privacy in chat

🔒 What you should know

  • Your display name and the icon of the app you listed are shown next to your messages inside the group.
  • Messages are stored on our servers only while the group is running, and are deleted automatically when the group finishes.
  • If a message is reported, a copy is kept in the moderation record so the admin can review it and recognise repeat offenders.
  • Screenshots of the chat screen are blocked inside the app.
  • Anything you type is visible to every member of your group. Treat it as public — do not type anything private.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

6

Think a decision was wrong?

If your chat access was suspended and you believe it was a mistake, email aitrickertesters@gmail.com with your registered email address and the group name. We will look at the reported message and reply within 24 hours. If the report was unfair, the suspension is lifted and the message is restored.

7

Changes to these Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines as the feature grows. When we make a meaningful change, the app asks you to read and accept the new version before you send your next message. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.
