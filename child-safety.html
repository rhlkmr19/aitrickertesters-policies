Child Safety Standards

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Our Commitment

AiTricker Testers is committed to the safety and protection of all users, particularly children. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation on our platform.

Platform Overview

AiTricker Testers is a developer collaboration platform designed for Android app developers to test each other's applications. The platform is intended for users aged 13 and above. Users between 13 and 18 must have parental or guardian consent. The app does not target children and does not feature any content directed at minors.

Prevention Measures

  • The platform does not allow users to share images, videos, or any media content with other users.
  • Testing groups include an optional text-only group chat, limited to the members of that group. It is used to coordinate testing. There is no private one-to-one messaging, no way for a stranger to contact a user, and no way to send photos, videos, files or voice notes — the feature accepts short text only (100 characters per message).
  • Every chat message is checked on our servers before it is delivered. Sexual content, abuse, links, contact details (phone numbers, email addresses, social handles) and promotional content are refused automatically, which prevents users from being directed to any off-platform channel.
  • Any member can report a message; enough reports hide it immediately and send it to the admin for review. Any member can also block another member. Admins can delete messages and suspend a user's chat access. Admins can also proactively open and review the chat of a running group — without waiting for a report — to look for abuse, grooming attempts, scams or attempts to move a conversation off the platform.
  • Group chat messages are deleted automatically when the group finishes, and are never publicly visible.
  • Other user-generated content is limited to app listings (app name, package name, description) which are monitored.
  • All users must authenticate via Google Sign-In, ensuring identity verification.
  • The platform does not collect or display personal information of users to other users beyond a display name.

Detection and Reporting

  • Any content or behavior suspected of involving child exploitation will be immediately removed and reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Users can report concerns via the in-app reporting mechanism (press and hold a chat message → Report, or the report option on an app listing) or by contacting us directly.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies in investigations related to child safety.

Enforcement Actions

  • Accounts found violating child safety standards will be permanently terminated without warning.
  • All associated data will be preserved for law enforcement purposes and reported to relevant authorities.
  • Violating users will be permanently banned from the platform.

Compliance

AiTricker Testers complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act (POCSO) — India
  • Applicable regional and national child protection laws
